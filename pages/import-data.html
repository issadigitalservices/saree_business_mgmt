Import Safety & Data Protection

✓ Original Product Codes

Historical PR product codes will remain unchanged. New products will continue the same sequence.

✓ No Double Stock Deduction

Historical sales will not reduce the inventory quantities already imported.

✓ Duplicate Protection

Existing product codes and receipt numbers will be checked before importing.

✓ Financial Data Preservation

Original financial values and identified inconsistencies will be retained for review.

✓ OPEX Unchanged

Operating expenses and salary records will not be imported or modified.

✓ Administrator Access

Historical imports are restricted to authorized administrator accounts.

3. Manage Historical Imports

Delete previously imported historical records before uploading corrected CSV files. Normal website transactions are protected.

Admin Only

Historical Inventory

Delete only products previously imported from the historical Inventory CSV.

Historical Sales

Delete only receipts previously imported from the historical Sales CSV. No inventory stock will be restored.

Warning: Deleted Firestore records cannot be recovered unless you have a backup. Export or back up your data before deleting.
